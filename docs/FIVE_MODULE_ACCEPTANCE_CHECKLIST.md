# Five-module requirement and acceptance checklist

Prepared 6 October 2026; refreshed 8 October 2026 from the maintained requirements and current implementation. This is a traceability inventory, not a blanket acceptance certificate.

## Scope and status

Only Dashboard/Timeline, Supervisor Appointments, Panel Appointments, Marks, and Workflow/Approval Tracking are owned. Semester/capacity, participant lifecycle, acting delegation, supporting supervision, research amendments and completion windows are included where they govern those five workflows; they do not create additional top-level modules. Registry, general Files, FAQ, Letters and Announcements ownership remains outside this checklist.

Each in-scope requirement bullet is retained verbatim below and linked to its source section. The current root requirements/design/status take precedence over historical review handoffs and older PDF wording. Older UI/backend-switch wording is read in conjunction with the later Backend-Only and Central Semester rules; apparent conflicts require documentation reconciliation, not reintroducing mocks or removing current workspaces.

- **Passed**: the evidence column identifies a completed, specific acceptance slice; its scope is limited to the requirement stated. Test evidence and browser evidence are distinguished.
- **Failed**: a currently reproducible deviation. Fixed original failures are recorded in the admin report and are not left as open failures.
- **Awaiting acceptance**: regression candidates exist or previous broad suites passed, but this checklist has not established complete scenario evidence for this clause. It does not mean the feature is absent.
- Faculty confirmation, coordinator identity policy and explicit outside-scope items stay **Awaiting acceptance** with a deferred/external note; no implementation is authorized by that label.
